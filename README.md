I'm a Data Scientist & Analytics Engineer with experience building data-driven solutions across finance, consulting, and the public sector. <br>
I design and deploy end-to-end analytics systems, from data ingestion and cleansing to machine learning, NLP, statistical modeling, and BI dashboards.<br>

## 🔭 My Interests:
I’m currently working on adding projects to my portfolio with a focus on:<br><br> 
- Data Profiling and Exploratory Data Analysis <br>
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)<br>
- Forecasting and Time Series for Prediction<br>
- Chatbots and Text Generating Models<br><br>

I’m passionate about Statistics & Probability, Data Science & Natural Language Processing (NLP)<br><br>
